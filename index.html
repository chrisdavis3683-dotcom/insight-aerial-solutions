Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Insight Aerial Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects client confidentiality and individual privacy across all digital and aerial operations.

1. Information Collected

We collect contact information (names, corporate emails, telephone numbers, and site addresses) submitted voluntarily via our quote intake forms. We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal or corporate information to third parties.

2. Aerial Operations & Incidental Capture

All flight operations are conducted strictly within FAA Part 107 safety regulations and targeted boundaries authorized by the client. Flight paths are planned to minimize incidental visual capture of adjacent private properties. Any uncontracted identifiable personal data inadvertently captured during data collection is excluded from final deliverables.

3. Data Retention & Client Ownership

Raw sensor data, orthomosaics, 3D point clouds, and inspection media are retained on secure local storage for client delivery and archival purposes. Deliverables are transferred directly to authorized clients.

Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 2026

By engaging Insight Aerial Solutions LLC for aerial imaging, data acquisition, or inspection operations, the client agrees to the following operating terms:

1. FAA Compliance & Airspace Authority

All operations are piloted by an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC). Operations are strictly contingent upon FAA airspace authorizations (LAANC/COA), weather minimums, and site safety evaluations. The RPIC maintains sole and absolute authority to delay, cancel, or reschedule flights to maintain safety and federal compliance.

2. Site Access & Authorization

Clients warrant that they own or have obtained legal permission and physical authorization to access the subject property and fly the airspace overhead for the requested scope of work.

3. Geospatial & Boundary Disclaimer

Aerial photogrammetry, 2D orthomosaic maps, digital elevation models, and 3D point clouds provided by Insight Aerial Solutions LLC are visual and relative spatial data deliverables engineered for visual analysis, planning, and progress tracking. They do not constitute certified legal boundary surveys or engineering stamped deliverables unless verified by a state-licensed surveyor or professional engineer.

4. Weather & Rescheduling

Flights postponed due to severe wind, precipitation, low cloud ceilings, or temporary airspace restrictions (TFRs) will be rescheduled for the earliest mutually viable weather window at no penalty.
