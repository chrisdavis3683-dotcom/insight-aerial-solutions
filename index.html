Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

Insight Aerial Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects client confidentiality and individual privacy across all digital, aerial, and terrestrial spatial operations.

1. Information Collected

We collect contact information (names, corporate emails, telephone numbers, and site addresses) submitted voluntarily via our quote intake forms. We do not sell, rent, or distribute corporate information to third parties.

2. Spatial Operations & Incidental Capture

All flight and interior capture operations are conducted strictly within client-authorized boundaries. Sensitive visual data (monitors, proprietary trade assets, or individual identifiers) inadvertently recorded during scanning can be digitally blurred upon client request.

3. Data Retention & Archival

Raw point cloud data, E57 files, orthomosaics, and 3D digital twins are stored on secure local and encrypted cloud storage for delivery and ongoing active hosting contracts.

Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 2026

By engaging Insight Aerial Solutions LLC for aerial imaging, terrestrial 3D laser scanning, or reality capture operations, the client agrees to the following operating terms:

1. Airspace & Field Authority

All drone operations are piloted by an FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC). Operations are strictly contingent upon FAA airspace authorizations, weather minimums, and site safety evaluations. The RPIC maintains sole authority to delay or reschedule flights to maintain safety and federal compliance.

2. Site Access & Authorization

Clients warrant that they own or have obtained legal permission and physical authorization to access the subject property and scan interior/exterior spaces for the requested scope of work.

3. Dimensional & Boundary Disclaimer

Spatial models, 3D point clouds (.E57, .XYZ), 2D schematic floor plans, and aerial orthomosaics provided by Insight Aerial Solutions LLC are high-precision visual and relative spatial data deliverables engineered for design planning, estimating, and progress tracking. They do not constitute certified legal boundary surveys or engineering stamped deliverables unless verified by a state-licensed surveyor or registered professional engineer.

4. Digital Twin Hosting Schedule

Standard reality capture projects include six (6) months of active cloud hosting from the date of delivery. Continued hosting thereafter is available under monthly or annual renewal agreements; unrenewed models are securely archived.
